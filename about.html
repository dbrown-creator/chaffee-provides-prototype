Connecting you to Chaffee County's agricultural community

Chaffee Provides helps people in the Upper Arkansas Valley find food grown and raised here: beef and lamb, eggs and dairy, vegetables, garlic, flowers and honey. It also lists the markets, restaurants and food programs that bring that food to the table.

Buying local does more than fill the fridge. It keeps working farms and ranches working, which protects open land, wildlife habitat and water quality in a fast-growing valley, and keeps more of every food dollar in the community.

Who we are

Chaffee Provides is a project of Guidestone Colorado, a Salida nonprofit building a strong agricultural future through education and partnerships, with Envision Chaffee County. The site launched in 2021, when pandemic supply-chain gaps showed how much the valley could feed itself.

Funded in part by the Chaffee County Community Foundation's Food Access Cohort and Chaffee Common Ground.

How we keep listings current

Directories go stale: markets move parks, farm stands change days, phone numbers change hands. This version of the site is built to avoid that:

  • Many sources, one listing. We combine the Chaffee Provides directory with Colorado Proud, the Colorado Farmers Market Association and the USDA Local Food Portal, and merge duplicates.
  • The provider's own word wins. We check each listing against the provider's own website. When sources disagree, the provider's site wins, then Colorado Proud, then the other directories.
  • Nothing is guessed. A detail only appears if a source states it. Every listing page shows where each detail came from and when it was last checked.
  • People correct, the system remembers. When a farmer or reader sends a fix, it's saved so future updates never overwrite it.
