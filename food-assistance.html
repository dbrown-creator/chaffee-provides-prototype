Food assistance in Chaffee County

Free meals, food pantries, a 24/7 community fridge and monthly food boxes in Salida and Buena Vista. No one should go hungry in a valley that grows this much food.

SNAP changes: if your SNAP benefits are delayed or reduced, the pantries and meals below are open to everyone. Many farmers' markets also match SNAP dollars — look for "Accepts SNAP" on the Find Food map.
